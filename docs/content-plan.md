## Strategy & Philosophy — `/strategy`

**Eyebrow:** OUR PHILOSOPHY
**H1:** Protect first. Grow always.

> Iron Hold Capital was built on a simple premise: most capital is lost not to bad
> markets, but to bad discipline. We combine three asset classes that rarely move
> together — digital assets, gold, and actively traded global markets — into a
> single, risk-managed approach.
>
> Each pillar plays a distinct role. Gold anchors the portfolio against
> uncertainty. Digital assets capture asymmetric, longer-horizon opportunity.
> Global trading strategies stay active where markets are inefficient enough to
> reward it. None of the three is a bet on a single outcome — together, they are
> built to hold their ground across cycles.

**How we think about risk** — position sizing, downside discipline, no
single-asset concentration. Written as principles, not numbers — no real
performance data exists yet, so none is implied or shown.
